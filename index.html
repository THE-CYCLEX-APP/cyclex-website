FAQ

What is CYCLEX?

CYCLEX is an app that adapts your training to your menstrual cycle. You log your cycle, CYCLEX works out which phase you're in, and it adjusts the intensity and order of your sessions to match.

Is CYCLEX a period tracker?

No. CYCLEX uses your cycle as an input, but its job is your training. Instead of only telling you which day you're on, it tells you what that means for today's session.

Do I have to change my training plan?

No. CYCLEX keeps your plan and helps you adapt it — heavier work goes where your body can take it, and lighter days protect your recovery.

Who is CYCLEX for?

Every active woman, from people who train twice a week to competitive athletes. You don't need to know anything about cycle science to start.

Is my data safe?

Your health and cycle data is encrypted and stored locally on your device. We are GDPR compliant and will never sell or share your data.

When can I use the app?

A script is a piece of program code that is used to make our website function properly and interactively. This code is executed on our server or on your device.

4. What is a web beacon?

A web beacon (or a pixel tag) is a small, invisible piece of text or image on a website that is used to monitor traffic on a website. In order to do this, various data about you is stored using web beacons.
